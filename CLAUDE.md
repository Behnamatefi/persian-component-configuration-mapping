## 1. What is Saraf?

**Saraf is a wealth management platform for ordinary Iranians**, not a crypto exchange. This distinction is critical and shapes everything.

The product lets regular people, with no financial expertise, manage and grow their monthly income through a simplified mobile app. Users can hold and trade across **multiple asset classes** in one place: crypto, ETFs, indices, leveraged products, commodities, and stocks. Every move is guided by AI-powered portfolio recommendations.

Saraf began as a price monitoring board for currency, gold, and stock rates. It grew into crypto trading. It is now a true **multi-asset investment hub** with the ambition of replacing how ordinary Iranians manage their wealth.

**Core philosophy:** *"The dream of simplicity in a complex world."*
Simplicity is a hard product constraint, not a preference. If a feature adds friction or complexity to the main flow, it does not belong there.

**Platforms:**
- Consumer app: [saraf.app](https://saraf.app)
- Trader platform: [trader.saraf.app](https://trader.saraf.app)
- Language: Persian (Farsi)
- Layout: Right-to-left (RTL)

## 2. Vision

Saraf's vision is built on three commitments:

**Radical simplicity.** Strip away the technical barriers of finance until the product is so essential it earns a permanent place on every user's smartphone.

**Market leadership.** Build the **largest investment market in Iran**. Become the default destination where Iranians come to manage wealth.

**Total tokenization.** Within the next five years, all liquid assets (gold, silver, foreign stocks, traditional funds) will be tokenized on Saraf, allowing 24/7 trading with the same ease as buying crypto today.

---

## 3. Mission

**Investment for everyone.** Any ordinary person should be able to protect and grow their monthly salary through intelligent management, regardless of how difficult the economy gets.

**Stability and reliability.** Build infrastructure with such precision that the financial sector can rely on it without interruption. Saraf is positioned as a "financial assistant" users can lean on for every economic decision.

**Education and empowerment.** Through Saraf Academy, raise financial literacy across the user base so even novices feel informed and supported before taking risks.

## 5. Product Overview

### Asset Classes Available
Saraf is a multi-asset platform. Users can trade and hold:

- **Crypto** (BTC, ETH, major altcoins)
- **ETFs** (exchange-traded funds)
- **Indices** (market index exposure)
- **Leveraged products** (leveraged tokens and instruments for amplified exposure)
- **Commodities** (gold tokenized as PAXG, with more planned)
- **Stocks** (Iranian and global equities, with global stocks expanding)
- **Iranian Toman** (fiat in/out)
- **Fixed-Income Fund** (~30% annual daily interest, no Sejam registration required, 24-hour buy/sell liquidity, supervised by Central Insurance)
- **Gift cards** (Toman or crypto denominations)

### Trading Tools
- **Limit Orders.** Set specific buy or sell prices instead of trading at current market rates.
- **Leverage.** Trade with amplified exposure on supported assets. Designed with risk warnings and guardrails for non-expert users.
- **Index Trading.** Get diversified market exposure through a single position rather than picking individual assets.
- **Auto-Buy / DCA.** Recurring automated purchases on daily, weekly, or monthly schedules.
- **PnL Sheet.** Portfolio performance tracking with target-based analysis.

### Intelligence Layer
- **AI Assistant** for personalized portfolio recommendations
- **Risk Assessment** (12-question survey to identify risk appetite, then suggests portfolio allocation, e.g., 50% gold, 25% BTC, 25% ETH)
- **Step-by-Step Onboarding Widget** (KYC, risk survey, deposit, first trade)
- **Saraf Academy** (financial literacy hub)
- **Saraf Bot** (AI chatbot, in development)

### Financial Services
- **Loans.** Asset-backed lending using crypto as collateral, via partners HiBank and AzkiVam.
- **Insurance.** Asset and activity-linked offerings.
- **Saraf Card** *(temporarily disabled due to banking partner issues)*. When live, this is a physical and digital bank card linked to the user's Saraf account, allowing instant liquidity and direct spending of investment returns at point-of-sale.

## 7. Competitive Positioning (Critical for AI Tools)

**Saraf does NOT compete with crypto exchanges.** This is the single most important positioning point.

Nobitex, Wallex, Ramzinex, and similar exchanges are **not Saraf's competitors**. They serve crypto-native traders. Saraf serves people who want their money to grow without learning what an order book is.

**Saraf's actual competitors are wealth managers and fund managers**, such as **Eco Ehsan** and similar firms in the Iranian asset management space. These are the businesses Saraf is taking users from and the category Saraf is competing inside.

**The breadth of asset classes (crypto, ETFs, indices, leverage, commodities, stocks) reinforces this positioning.** Saraf is not a single-product trading venue. It is a full wealth management surface where any liquid asset can be held, tracked, and grown.

**What this means for AI-generated content:**
- Never frame Saraf as a "crypto exchange" or position it alongside trading platforms
- Frame Saraf as a **wealth management** or **multi-asset investment** platform
- Comparisons should be drawn against fund managers and asset managers, not crypto venues
- The narrative is: *"Manage your money intelligently across every asset class"*, not *"Trade crypto easily"*
- Crypto is one of many assets on Saraf, not the identity of Saraf

## 8. Constraints for AI Tools Generating Content

When generating any content, copy, recommendations, or assets for Saraf:

**Tone and voice.**
- Speak to non-experts. No jargon without inline explanation.
- Reassuring, calm, professional. Saraf is a financial assistant, not a hype machine.
- Persian-first. Anything written in English should be considered a draft for translation.

**Simplicity rule.**
- If the response or feature requires more than a glance to understand, it fails the simplicity test.
- Defer complexity behind a "go deeper" link. Keep main flows clean.

**Financial honesty.**
- Never state guaranteed returns. The Fixed-Income Fund's ~30% annual rate is approximate and subject to change.
- Avoid language that could be read as investment advice or a guarantee of profit.
- Always frame projections as possibilities, not promises.
- For leveraged products specifically, always pair benefits with clear risk language. Leverage amplifies losses as well as gains.

**Positioning consistency.**
- Always position Saraf as wealth management for ordinary people.
- Never compare to crypto exchanges. Never lead with crypto as the identity.
- Lead with the outcome (growing wealth, beating inflation, simple investing), not the mechanism (trading, swaps, order types).

**RTL and Persian context.**
- All copy must work in right-to-left layout.
- Cultural and economic references should be Iran-specific where relevant (Toman, Sejam, local regulatory bodies).
